# Retention Policy

No product database exists yet.

The browser-local Noesis workspace remains until the user clears site data,
uses the two-step reset, or overwrites it with a validated import. Audit history
is capped at 200 events and receipts at 25 records. There is no server backup or
recovery guarantee.

Future retention policies must define:

- Data category.
- Owner.
- Retention window.
- Deletion process.
- Backup implications.
- Legal or compliance constraints.
